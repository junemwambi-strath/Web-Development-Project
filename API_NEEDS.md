Reflection Paragraph
The partner interview helped us better understand how our application could interact with the inventory and sales management system by team 11. What surprised us most was the amount of useful information their application already manages that could be relevant to our own app. Although the two applications serve different purposes, we identified several areas of overlap, particularly in product stock, individual sales, payments, and sales reports. The interview was also helpful because our upstream partner was receptive and willing to explain how their system works, which allowed us to ask questions and explore possible ways of using their resources. Through the discussion, we realized that creating an API connection is not simply about accessing data, but about identifying specific data that can support meaningful features in our application.
